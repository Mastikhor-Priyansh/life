WHAT I THINK
ABOUT HER

Maybe I don't have the perfect words. But I know how she makes me feel.

“

Sach bolun toh, mujhe nahi pata main usko perfectly kaise describe karun. Like, jab bhi main usko dekhta hoon, pata nahi kyun, but woh mujhe ekdum beautiful lagti hai. Kabhi kabhi toh aisa lagta hai jaise koi angel Earth pe aa gaya ho.

Aur sirf uski looks ki baat nahi hai. Uska cute sa behavior, uski little expressions, jis tarah woh chhoti chhoti cheezein karti hai... mujhe woh sab bahut pasand hai. Pata nahi, woh jab apne normal way mein behave karti hai, tab bhi mujhe somehow woh bahut cute lagti hai.

But agar mujhe ek cheez choose karni ho jo mujhe usmein sabse zyada pasand hai, toh probably uski eyes. Uski eyes ekdum expressive hain. Aisa lagta hai jaise woh hamesha thodi si shine karti rehti hain. Aur main jab uski eyes mein dekhta hoon na, toh bas... pata nahi. Fida ho jaata hoon us pe. Har baar thoda sa aur.

Waise uski height 5'3" hai, jo honestly bilkul perfect hai. But pata nahi kyun, mere saamne woh phir bhi itni si hi lagti hai. Bas ye meri taraf se thoda sa cute sa mazaak hai — height ko lekar koi complaint bilkul nahi.

Uske kuch naam maine apne liye rakhe hain... woh main yahan disclose nahi karunga. Kuch cheezein private hi achhi lagti hain. Aur honestly, uska jo actual naam hai na, mujhe woh bhi bahut achha lagta hai. Uska naam screen pe dikh jaaye, ya bas uske baare mein sochun, toh ek alag si happiness feel hoti hai.

· · ♥ · ·

Shayad main usko perfectly describe kabhi na kar paun. Main uski smile ki baat kar sakta hoon, eyes ki, uske behavior ki, uski voice ki, uski little habits ki, aur countless chhoti chhoti cheezon ki. But end mein simple answer shayad yehi hai — whenever I see her, I just feel happy.

Some feelings are easier to feel than to explain.
